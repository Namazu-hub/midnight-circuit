# v4.9.3 — Stage 1 lift and wiring pass

The first tool lift now unlocks a real upper route: a high insulated cabinet
blocks the ground path, so PICO must ride the paid lift and jump across. The
first ride costs ¥10; retry rides are free to avoid a checkpoint softlock.
Stage 1 Monster Energy pickups cost ¥10 apiece and remain available if funds
or inventory space are insufficient. Defeated Stage 1 enemies return after an
18-second minimum once their original spawn is off camera, with no second
research-fund reward.

Obsolete pulse-only platforms were replaced with honest fixed decking. Server
archive wall material continues past the end boundary and down the shaft. The
single required wiring puzzle now displays three jacketed cables, terminal
clamps and a lit output conduit in the actual visible camera area. Its optional
server-room hint and floor-button order remain intact.

See `docs/STAGE1-MAP-v4.9.md` for geometry, retry conditions and validation.
